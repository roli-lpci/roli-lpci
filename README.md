## Background

Philosophy of language (Wittgenstein, Gadamer, Heidegger) applied to AI systems. The traditions that spent centuries studying how meaning breaks down have a lot to say about why LLMs fail the way they do.

**LPCI** — Linguistically Persistent Cognitive Interface — the idea that persistent language between human and AI creates a shared cognitive space that evolves over time.
